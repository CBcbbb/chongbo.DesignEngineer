A · Zinc

The system's own neutral, with the scales lit like a variables panel

The field is zinc, the shadcn neutral the system was built on, in both modes. Colour only comes from the six scales, five steps each, shown with their names like Figma's variables. Openers are on zinc; evidence sits on white or zinc as each mode needs. Cool and precise, a long way from Nike's warm black and orange.

C · The page wears the customer's brand (recommended)

The cover takes a customer's colour, font and button shape, and stays readable

The project's thesis becomes its identity: the field is a brand colour, and "Seen as" switches the whole case file between the platform default and three invented customers. Text on the field turns dark or light by the system's own rule, so a pale yellow customer still reads. The default is a deep teal, apart from Components for Any Data's blue and HHCD's green.
