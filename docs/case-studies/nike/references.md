# Nike: references that shaped the prototype

Links checked on 10 Oct 2026.

1. **The reader handles the real thing, introduced by one line.** Emil Kowalski's [Building a drawer component](https://emilkowal.ski/ui/building-a-drawer-component) drops a working recreation of Family's drawer into the article under a short "Try it out" prompt, rather than a video of it. → The shoebox animation plays in place, and the product card is a slider the reader drags.
2. **Labels on live demos are instructions.** Rauno Freiberg's [Interaction design essay](https://rauno.me/craft/interaction-design) labels its radial-menu demo with what to do ("Hold and rotate"), not what it is. → Interactive captions start with the action: "Drag to compare", "Play", "See it at 0:25".
3. **One product frame, repeated, ties the sections together.** [Granola's homepage](https://www.granola.ai) shows the same meeting window before, during and after a call. → One phone frame and one black field carry the recording; every card's "See it at…" link returns to that same frame.

## Granola, studied in the browser (10 Oct 2026)

- **Claim left, reasoning right.** Feature rows set a large regular-weight serif heading (Quadrant, 32–48 px) on the left and a short sans paragraph (Melange, 16 px) on the right. → Each card head now puts index, kind and claim left, and body, tags and action right; headings use Geist at weight 500 rather than bold.
- **Product UI floats on a rich backdrop.** Screens sit in rounded windows (16–28 px radii) over landscape photos and textured collage, never on flat grey. → Evidence sits on a grain gradient in Nike orange and black, echoing the grainy gradient of Chongbo's original cover; radii went up to 20 px (sheets) and 14 px (mounts).
- **One brand colour, used for action.** Olive (#5b6f00) appears only on download buttons; sections alternate white, warm off-white (#f7f7f2) and charcoal (#292929). → Paper, black and Nike orange alternate; orange marks the cover, part openers and every control.
