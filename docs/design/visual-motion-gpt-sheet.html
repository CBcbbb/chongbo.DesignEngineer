0 · 风格说明（每个新对话先发这一段）

不出图
作用
让 GPT 在整个对话里记住同一种光、材质和规矩。之后每条提示词都在这个对话里接着发，一个对话可以连做好几张。
You are making images for a personal website built around one photographed object: a low, wide 1970s Italian-style desktop printer with a warm cream body, softly rounded ends, a ribbed dark rubber base, a dark green-tinted display window in a polished frame, and a glossy red key (close to #c9372a) in a knurled chrome ring.

For every image in this conversation:
- Photographic, premium and calm, like a high-end product catalogue. Real materials, fine detail, small real-world imperfections.
- One large softbox from the upper left, gentle falloff, no lens flare, no dramatic shadows.
- Warm neutral colours: studio grey close to #e9e7e2, paper close to #fbfaf7, ink close to #1c1b19. Red is the only strong colour.
- Never any text, numbers, logos, watermarks or symbols, unless I ask for them.
- When I give a background colour, make it flat and exactly that colour, with nothing else in it.

Reply "Ready" and wait for my first request.

9 · 出现偏差时用的修正语

不出图
作用
GPT 改多了、加了字、换了角度时，在同一个对话里回它这一句。
That changed too much. Keep everything exactly as in the attached original: the same camera, framing, proportions, materials and lighting. Make only the change I asked for, and add no text, logos or new parts.

GPT 不适合做的：所有会动的东西（按键、出纸、屏幕、读取光，都是代码）；精确的颜色和无缝拼接（我在后期调）；分享图（你说不需要，不做了）。
