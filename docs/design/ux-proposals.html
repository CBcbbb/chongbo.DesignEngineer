Proposal 01 · Covers

Covers, made as one set

Round one's covers were whatever each project had: a full screenshot of the map, a crop of a cover's corner, a render. Side by side they don't belong together, and at the home row's size (92px wide) the screenshot is noise. Proposed: one rule for all four, built from each case file's own cover art.

  • One frame16:10, a 6px radius, no text and no interface inside. The title sits under the cover, never on it.
  • One field, one drawingThe project's colour field from its case file, and the art its cover already uses: the map's four marks, the wires, the layers, the shoebox.
  • One sizeEach drawing centred, filling about two-thirds of the height. Covers are objects: the same colours in light and dark, like the printer.

At the home row's size (on hold)

A check that the set still reads at 92px. Nothing here is to be built yet.

The details

  • On the studio, after the last card, under one hairline: the shell's colours and Geist, never the project's.
  • Capitals at 700, tracking −0.03em, balanced over at most two lines. Long names wrap: "One Design System, Three Products, Any Brand".
  • The arrow: a 28px stub at rest. On hover it draws all the way to the name in 0.7s: a wave, a bow or, now and then, a small loop. Its head follows the line's last direction and is drawn in 0.24s. On leaving it draws back.

Why

  • Every case file ends the same way, so the site reads as one piece.
  • The arrow is the back pill's line, turned forward: it already exists in Pill.tsx (BACK_LINES), unused since the nav went plain.
  • Playbook §7 keeps play out of case files. This control sits outside the last card and belongs to the shell; I'd add one line to §7 saying so.

Cost

Half a day: one NextCase component, used by CaseFile.tsx and NikeCase.tsx (shared files, via SHARED-CHANGES.md), with the arrow as its own small component so 02, 04 and 07 reuse it. Phones: it draws once when it comes into view. Reduced motion: the full arrow, still.

The wording

"AI does the finding. I did the work." Plain, a little dry, and short enough to read in passing. My pick.

"The printer runs on AI. The work it prints is mine." Ties the note to the machine on the home page; longer.

"AI fetches the pages, like a good dog. The work on them is mine." The funniest, and the joke carries the meaning: it fetches, it doesn't make. Maybe one wink too many next to the hammer and the eyes.

"Ask the machine anything. It only knows what I've made." Says how it works (it chooses only from my work), but claims the work less directly.

Today: name, role, city and email on one line, and the note in 12px mono under it.

All four are honest: AI helps people find the work, and the work is mine. None claims the words.

The motion

Hover the note: a red line is drawn under the claim, like a signature (one of three strokes, 0.45s, drawn back on leaving). The red is the button's red.

Copy the address: a small slip prints up out of the button saying "Copied", in the printer's stepped motion, and feeds back down. The address itself opens an email.

Proposal 07 · The system

One arrow for every way on

Proposed: every "go somewhere" uses the same drawn arrow, at rest a stub and on hover drawn out, so the site's controls feel made by one hand. The hammer, the eyes and the asterisk stay as they are.

The rules

  • One stroke: 1.8, round ends, as the nav icons.
  • Short arrows: a wave or a bow, picked at random, 0.3s. The next case file's long arrow can also loop, 0.7s.
  • The head follows the line's last direction. Focus does what hover does.

Where it stops

Not inside case cards: their controls stay serious (playbook §7). The sheets' foot and the next case file are the shell's, so they qualify.

Cost

Half a day for the component and the swaps, once 03 exists. Pill.tsx's BACK_LINES becomes the shared source.
