The system behind these screens

Paper, ink and one stamp red

A card catalogue, not a costume. The object gives the site its logic: work is filed as cards, a question pulls cards out, and each project is a short case file. Texture comes from type, rules and shadow, never from images of paper.

Type · two faces

Newsreader for reading Headlines, case files and answers. A newspaper serif with optical sizes, so it stays crisp at 72px and calm at 19px. IBM Plex Mono for what's typed and filed The question you type, labels, tabs and metadata. Typewriter feel without the costume of Courier.

Motion · three moments only

  1. Settle. On load, the cards in the drawer settle by a few pixels, once. 600ms.
  2. File and pull. Your question card slides into the drawer while cards flick behind it; the answer card rises and turns to face you. The flicking covers the wait for the answer.
  3. Deal. In the drawer view, cards are dealt onto the table in order, 60ms apart.

All three switch off with reduced motion: cards simply appear. Total time from asking to reading: under 1.5 seconds plus the answer.
